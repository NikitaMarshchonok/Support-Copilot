# Change Dates Policy (Demo)

## Summary
- Date changes depend on rate type and availability.
- Some bookings require host approval.
- Fees may apply if outside the free change window.

## Rules
1) Confirm booking ID and current dates.
2) Check availability for requested dates.
3) If rate is flexible and within window, process change.
4) If non‑refundable or restricted, explain policy and offer alternatives.

## Required info
- Booking ID
- Current dates
- Requested new dates

## Suggested wording
"I can help check availability and the change policy once I have the booking ID and requested dates."
