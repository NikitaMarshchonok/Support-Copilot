# Price Mismatch (Demo)

## Summary
- Validate displayed price vs final charged price.
- Check taxes/fees/currency conversions.
- Only promise adjustments when policy allows.

## Rules
1) Ask for screenshots of the listed price and final charge.
2) Verify currency and tax/fee breakdown in the booking record.
3) If mismatch is confirmed and policy allows, offer adjustment or credit.
4) If not confirmed, explain the breakdown and close the case.

## Required info
- Booking ID
- Screenshots (listing and payment confirmation)
- Currency and total charged amount

## Suggested wording
"Please share screenshots of the listed price and your payment confirmation so I can verify a mismatch."
