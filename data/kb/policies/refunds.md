# Refunds Policy (Demo)

## Summary
- Refund eligibility depends on rate type and cancellation window.
- Partial refunds may apply for fees or non‑refundable components.
- Chargeback risk cases should be escalated.

## Rules
1) Confirm booking details and rate type before discussing refund amounts.
2) If within free‑cancellation window, process full refund.
3) If outside window or non‑refundable, explain policy and offer allowed alternatives.
4) If payment dispute / chargeback risk, escalate to L2 and log evidence.

## Required info
- Booking ID
- Amount paid and currency
- Payment method
- Cancellation timestamp

## Suggested wording
"I can help check refund eligibility once I confirm the booking ID, dates, and rate type."
